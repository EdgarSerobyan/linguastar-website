Practice speaking Spanish with an AI tutor

You’re checking in at a hotel. You know the word for a room, but how do you say you’ve already booked one? Try this exchange from LinguaStar on iPhone.

At the hotel desk

The tutor asks “¿Tienes una reserva?” (Do you have a reservation?) Try answering out loud for a two-night stay.

In the app, the learner replies “Sí, reservo una habitación para dos noches.” The tutor understands the booking and suggests a change to the verb:

Sí, reservé una habitación para dos noches.

“Yes, I booked a room for two nights.” Reservé puts the booking in the past. Reservo means “I book.”
