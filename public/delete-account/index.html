Delete your account

LinguaStar is provided by iSolutions OÜ.

Delete your account in the app

In LinguaStar, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@linguastar.ai with the email address associated with your LinguaStar account. If you signed in with Apple and used Hide My Email, include your Apple relay address if you know it. Keep your password and verification codes private.

What gets deleted

Account deletion removes your profile, learning progress, conversation history, and voice recordings from LinguaStar. We may retain information as needed to meet legal obligations. See the Privacy Policy for details.
